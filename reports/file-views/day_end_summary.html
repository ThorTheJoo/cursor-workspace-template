Day End Summary

Frequency: Daily · Parser: integrated

Single source of truth for one trading day: combined forecourt + shop sales, fuel volume, GP%, cashier exceptions, wet-stock EOD, and nett takings.

Key fields
  • batch_number
  • batch_date
  • nett_takings
  • fuel_total
  • fuel_volume
  • shop_total_incl
  • cash_variance
  • fuel_grades (pump vs tank variance)
  • top_categories
Valuable insights
  • Daily revenue trend and day-on-day deltas
  • Fuel litres sold vs wet-stock variance (loss/theft/dip error)
  • Shop category mix and GP%
  • Cash control at shift close
Owner callouts
  • Cash variance > R50 — investigate cashier/till
  • Fuel pump-tank variance > ±25 L per grade — wet stock alert
  • Nett takings drop >30% vs prior day — trading anomaly (Sunday, supply, outage)

Reconciles with: eft_batch_summary, bank_feed.speedpoint, ocr_whatsapp.fuel_volume
